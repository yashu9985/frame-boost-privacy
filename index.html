Privacy Policy for Frame Boost

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frame Boost ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We built Frame Boost to process your videos directly on your device — we do not operate our own servers that receive or store your video files.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Frame Boost does not require you to create an account, does not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers directly from you. Videos you select for processing are read from your device's local storage, processed locally using on-device video processing, and saved back to your device's gallery. Your video files are never uploaded to any server operated by us.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Media / Storage access: to let you select a video to process and save the processed result back to your device's gallery.
  • Internet access: required to display advertisements (see below).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Frame Boost displays ads using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, including but not limited to:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads

This information is collected and used by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy, not directly by us. You can review how Google handles this data here: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You may also review Google's information on how AdMob works at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Some features in the App are unlocked by watching a rewarded video ad. If you choose not to watch an ad, those specific features simply remain unavailable — no personal data is required to use the App's core functionality.

4. Children's Privacy

Frame Boost is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Retention

Since we do not operate servers that store your videos or personal data, there is no data retained by us beyond what is described above regarding advertising services. Processed videos remain on your own device under your control, and you may delete them at any time using your device's gallery or file manager.

6. Third-Party Services

Aside from Google AdMob, the App may rely on standard Google Play services required for Android apps to function (such as Google Play Services). These operate under Google's own privacy policy linked above.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding data collected by third-party services like AdMob, including the right to opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, on most Android devices).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
