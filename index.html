Privacy Policy for FrameSync

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FrameSync ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. FrameSync processes your videos directly on your device. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your video files.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

FrameSync does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers directly from you. Videos you select for processing are read from your device's storage, processed locally on your device, and saved back to your device's gallery. Your video files are never uploaded to any server operated by us.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Media / Storage access: to let you select a video to process and to save the processed result to your device's gallery.
  • Internet access: required to display advertisements (see below).

3. Temporary Files

While a video is being processed, the App creates a temporary output file on your device. These temporary files stay on your device only and are automatically deleted by the App after 24 hours. Videos you save to your gallery remain under your control, and you can delete them at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FrameSync displays ads using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, including but not limited to:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads

This information is collected and used by Google in accordance with its own privacy policy, not directly by us. You can review how Google handles this data here: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and about AdMob at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Some features in the App are unlocked by watching a rewarded video ad. If you choose not to watch an ad, those features remain unavailable. No personal data is required to use the App's core functionality.

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the consent options provided by Google or through your device's ad settings.

6. Children's Privacy

FrameSync is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention

Since we do not operate servers that store your videos or personal data, we retain no data beyond what is described above. Data collected by advertising services is retained according to Google's own policies.

8. Third-Party Services

Aside from Google AdMob, the App relies on standard Google Play services required for Android apps to function. These operate under Google's own privacy policy linked above.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding data collected by third-party services like AdMob, including the right to opt out of personalized advertising. On most Android devices you can do this in Settings → Google → Ads, where you can opt out of ads personalization or reset your advertising ID.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
